CCN agent free-form language

SubjectNet

Decides whether a motion part of a sentence is about the object being moved ("the drone should rise 4 metres") or about something else ("Mia turned to her brother"), so that the motion reader keeps only the right parts.

65,634 numbers trained

Tensor algebras (TensorNets)

owNet

The net built from scratch in Unit 4: it reads a short expression such as (a⨁b)⨂c, token by token, and writes the distributed form in prefix order (⨁ ⨂ a c ⨂ b c).

59,950 numbers trained in Wolfram (Unit 4)

tnParserNet

Parses a tensor-algebra expression: reads its tokens and writes its tree in prefix order, for the exact engine to check and use.

1,422,870 numbers drawn

tnPolicyNet

Simplifies: given an expression, chooses which rewrite rule to apply and at which node, so that the exact engine can carry it out soundly.

611,468 numbers drawn

tnValueNet

Evaluates: estimates three numbers about an expression (such as its dimension and its number of terms), checked against exact values from the symbolic engine.

626,819 numbers drawn

Fabric: anomaly detection

WeaveNet

Learns the regular criss-cross weave of normal fabric, so that whatever it cannot redraw (holes, loose threads, runs, knots) shows up as the defect: the learnt counterpart of removing the weave with an FFT notch filter.

8,464 numbers work in progress
